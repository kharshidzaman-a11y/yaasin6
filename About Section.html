Codingal is on a mission to inspire school kids to fall in love in coding. Coding helps develop logical thinking and problem solving skills. Coding jobs are the future. They already constitute more than 60% of all the jobs in science, techology, engineering and math. While still in school, those who start young will be ahead of everyone by the time they go to collage. They will be creators of the future.


In today's age, coding has became an essential skill. Coding for kids helps them interact with techology better. Studies also show that it aids in holistic development of the child, impoving cognitive functioning as well as logical thinking. holistic development means evoling congnitive , physical, and social skills of a child to prepare them better for the interactions with the world. Coding as an activity not only involves mental development but also focues on social understaning.
